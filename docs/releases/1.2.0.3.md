# xrncal Desktop 1.2.0.3

本版由 immoke 维护。原项目由 Xernnn 创建，采用 MIT 许可证。
[原项目](https://github.com/Xernnn/xrncal-desktop) · [本版源代码](https://github.com/immoke/xrncal-desktop/tree/v1.2.0.3)

## 日历界面

- 主日历和侧栏小日历支持下拉选择年份、月份，以及一键回到今天。中文年月采用「2027年 02月」格式。
- 全天日程使用对应日历的底色。定时日程使用左侧彩色短条，背景跟随浅色或暗色主题。
- 日程编辑、重复结束日期、搜索结果和拖动确认使用配置的日期格式。Windows 默认读取系统短日期格式。

## 同步与时区

- 「立即同步」同步所有已配置的账户和在线 ICS 订阅。下拉菜单也可单独同步某个账户或订阅，CalDAV 账户下的日历共用一个入口。
- 改善暗色模式下同步进行中、成功和失败时的文字对比度。
- 修复 ICS 时区条目缺少偏移规则时的时间偏移，例如北京时间被误当成 UTC。升级后首次同步会重新解析已同步的 CalDAV 日程和在线 ICS 订阅，即使内容未变。

## 重复日程

- 打开某次重复日程后，直接保存只影响本次。点击「修改系列」可查看原始开始日期、编辑规则并更新整个系列。
- 改进每周指定星期、每月指定日期和「每 3 个月的第 3 个星期三」等重复规则及结束日期的识别。
- 拖动普通重复日程改期时，可选择仅本次、本次及以后或整个系列。移动月度或年度系列时，未手动修改的结束日期会随系列调整。

## 下载与升级

- 提供 Windows x64 安装版、Windows x64 绿色 ZIP 和 Linux x64 AppImage。三个包均包含原项目的 MIT 许可证与第三方许可声明。
- 绿色版解压后运行 `xrncal.exe`。覆盖升级前请退出程序，保留同目录的 `xrncal.sqlite`。
- 已安装旧构建的 1.2.0.3 用户可从本页手动下载更新后的同版本安装包。
- 界面继续提供简体中文、英语和越南语。中文采用中国农历，越南语采用越南农历。

### Calendar interface

- Pick the year and month from dropdowns in the main calendar and sidebar mini calendar. Jump back to today in one click. The Simplified Chinese interface shows the year before the month.
- All-day events have a calendar-colored fill. Timed events use a short color marker on the left and a background that follows the light or dark theme.
- Date fields, recurrence end dates, search results, and drag confirmations use the configured date format. On Windows, the default comes from the system's short date format.

### Sync and time zones

- **Sync now** refreshes all configured accounts and online ICS subscriptions. Its menu can sync one account or subscription, with one entry per CalDAV account.
- Improved the readability of the progress button and success and failure messages in dark mode.
- Fixed time shifts when an ICS time-zone entry names a zone but omits its offset rules. The first sync after upgrading reparses previously synced CalDAV events and online ICS subscriptions, even when their content is unchanged.

### Recurring events

- Saving an opened occurrence changes that occurrence only. **Edit series** opens the original start date and recurrence rule for changes to the whole series.
- Improved support for rules such as selected weekdays, a specific day of the month, or the third Wednesday every three months. The editor also shows the recurrence end date.
- When dragging a regular occurrence, choose this occurrence, this and following occurrences, or the whole series. Moving a monthly or yearly series moves its end date when that date has been left unchanged in the editor.

### Downloads and upgrade

- Available as a Windows x64 installer, a Windows x64 portable ZIP, and a Linux x64 AppImage. All three include the original MIT License and third-party notices.
- To use the portable build, extract the ZIP and run `xrncal.exe`. Before overwriting an older build, quit the app and keep `xrncal.sqlite` in the same folder.
- If you already installed an earlier build of 1.2.0.3, download the updated package from this release page.
- Simplified Chinese, English, and Vietnamese remain available. The Chinese and Vietnamese interfaces use their respective lunar calendars.
